What happens after you send your requirement

  1. Sent: being read
  2. Day 2: a written reply
  3. Day 3: a proof on your data
  4. Day 5+: scope and price agreed first
Sent: being read

It lands with a director.

Phil or Alex reads everything you send: the brief, the screenshots, the recordings and the tricky example.

Day 2: a written reply

A written route

If we can direct you towards existing software that achieves what you want, we'll be happy to do that. We're happy to be useful, even if it doesn't lead to work for us.

Day 3: a proof on your data

A working proof

If it's not clear how to achieve the brief, we'll recommend a proof. The uncertain part runs on your representative data, and you get a fixed build quote at the end of day three.

Day 5+: scope and price agreed first

A full build

For a clear brief. We'll agree the scope and price before starting. We'll test it against your real cases. Live with permissions and an audit trail. We keep it maintained after launch.
