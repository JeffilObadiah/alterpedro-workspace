# SPRINT DRAFT: DEC-001 CREATIVE (OGILVY)

Status: DRAFT. Not open. Owner of all tasks: OGILVY until creative bots exist. Nothing here has a price, date or benchmark; those stay blank until the inputs below arrive.

## Blocking inputs (only the human can supply)
- OPEN-001: the outcome the client wants and the number that should move (Rule 001).
- The product and its single strongest proof (a number, measurement or visible feature). Without it, no headline and no video brief can be written.
- Who produces the video, the budget, and the delivery date (A8).
- The niche and the top 3 competitors, for the benchmark.

## Tasks
- C1: Product-proof brief. Name the one benefit and the evidence that shows it. Depends on: human input.
- C2: Headlines. Write at least 10 before choosing; list all 10 in the sprint report. Pick 1 for the test. Depends on: C1.
- C3: Video brief and script. The video demonstrates the proof from C1 and must make its point with the sound off. Depends on: C1, C2.
- C4: Poster frame art direction. The poster is the LCP element, so it must carry the same message as the video's first second. Depends on: C3. Hands the poster spec to TORVALDS (E2).
- C5: Video production. Producer, cost and date unknown (A8). Depends on: C3, human input on producer and budget.
- C6: Page copy and SEO. Headline and subhead are live HTML text, not text baked into the image or video. Add video captions and a transcript. Depends on: C2.
- C7: A/B test design. Hypothesis, one primary metric (A7), sample size and stop rule fixed before launch (with TORVALDS and DEMING). Depends on: OPEN-001, baseline numbers.
- C8: Benchmark. Compare the hero against the top 3 competitors on mobile LCP, hero payload, time to first video frame (TORVALDS measures) and on message clarity (OGILVY scores). Depends on: competitors named.

## Sequencing
C1 -> C2 -> C3 -> C4 and C5 in parallel with C6 -> C7 -> hand-off to TORVALDS E3/E6 -> launch -> single read-out at the agreed sample size.

## Acceptance criteria (written before work starts)
- CA1: The headline names one specific number, measurement or feature from C1.
- CA2: At least 10 headlines are written and listed before one is chosen.
- CA3: The video makes the benefit clear with the sound off.
- CA4: The poster and the video's first second carry the same message.
- CA5: The headline is live text in the HTML. Captions and a transcript exist.
- CA6: The primary metric (A7), sample size and stop rule are written in DECISIONS.md before launch.
- CA7: Every claim in the headline and video can be shown to be true (no claim without proof).

- Evidence tiers for CA3: this is an internal test, not T1-T4. If challenged, test it with real viewers.
